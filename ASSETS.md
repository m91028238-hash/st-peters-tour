### Small domes of the aisles and chapels
Each named cupola uses its own looking-up photograph. ### Photographs projected onto the statues (v5; assets/statues/*_c.jpg and lo/*_c.jpg)
Each statue's colour map is made from the real photograph(s) below: the camera of each photo was solved from landmarks (solvePnP), the sculpted mesh was warped to the photo's proportions, and the photo was projected onto the mesh with visibility, view-angle and feathered-edge weights, masked against the background, de-lit (low-frequency shading divided out), colour-matched and blended into one UV texture; areas no photo sees are filled from neighbouring texels and a matched marble/bronze colour (tools/photoproj). ## Modelled for this tour (no outside assets)
- **Architecture, baldacchino with its winged angels, Papal Altar, Confessio, crossing-pier loggias (Solomonic columns, balconies, carved relief panels), chapel aedicules, altars, candlesticks and railings** — geometry written in code (js/basilica.js); textures drawn at runtime from the marble swatches above (js/textures.js). Latin frieze text is Matthew 16:18–19 (Vulgate), public domain.
- **Statues: bronze St Peter, St Longinus, St Helena, St Veronica, St Andrew, the six nave founder saints and the baldacchino angels** (assets/statues/*.glb, *_n.jpg, *_ao.jpg, peter_c.jpg; lighter mobile versions in assets/statues/lo/) — modelled for this tour from scratch. No open-licence, sign-in-free 3D scans of these works were found, so each figure was sculpted procedurally as a signed-distance model (tools/sculpt: anatomy, faces, hair and beards, hands, drapery folds, attributes such as the keys, spear, cross, veil, X-cross, books and staffs), posed by eye from the reference photographs listed above (v4: founders re-posed with contrapposto, corrected hand attributes, mantles, and their real companion figures — the putti beside St Teresa, St Philip Neri and St Benedict and the trampled figure of Heresy under St Ignatius, all modelled from scratch; faces refined with lids, brows and lip structure), meshed with marching cubes, decimated in Blender 4.2 (GPL, used as a tool only) and given its own baked tangent-space normal map and ambient-occlusion map (plus a patina/wear colour map for the bronze St Peter). Compressed with meshopt (gltf-transform, MIT). The meshes are original work for this tour; since v5 their colour maps are projected from the photographs listed above (the four baldacchino angels keep a plain gilt material).
- The earlier photo-relief proxies and painted-out photo backdrops (assets/statues/*.bin, *_a.png, *_bg.jpg and the statue photos) were removed; the crossing niches are now marble shells.
- **St Peter setting, 1871 brocatello mosaic dossal** — cropped from “Saint Peter's Basilica (25).jpg”, CC BY-SA 3.0 (peter_brocade.jpg). <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saint_Peter%27s_Basilica_(25).jpg>
- **St Peter canopy valance (tiara, keys and tassels)** — cropped from the same photograph (peter_canopy.jpg, peter_canopy_side.jpg).
- **Pius IX mosaic medallion and gilt putti** — cropped and alpha-masked from “Algunas esculturas en la Basílica de San Pedro 38.jpg” by Álvaro de la Paz Franco, CC0 (peter_piusix.png). <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Algunas_esculturas_en_la_Bas%C3%ADlica_de_San_Pedro_38.jpg>
- **Marble throne, side palmette relief** — cropped from “Pilgrim at St Peter Enthroned.jpg” by Ethan Doyle White, CC BY-SA 4.0 (peter_throne.jpg). <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pilgrim_at_St_Peter_Enthroned.jpg>
- The 1757 stepped pedestal, the marble throne (shaped back, scrolled arms), the pierced gilt nimbus, the 1871 wall-mounted canopy tester and the barley-twist stands are modelled in 3D. The Latin on the tablet is drawn in type, lettered from the photograph: PIO IX PONT MAX / QVI PETRI ANNOS / IN PONTIFICATV ROMANO / VNVS AEQVAVIT / CLERVS VATICANVS / SACRAM ORNAVIT SEDEM / XVI KAL QVINT A MDCCCLXXI.
