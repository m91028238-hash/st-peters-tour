St Peter’s Basilica

A 3D walking tour of the greatest church of Christendom, built over the tomb of the Apostle Peter on the Vatican Hill.

Use the ring at the bottom left to walk, drag anywhere else to look around and up into the dome, and pinch or use + and − to zoom. Tap a gold i to read about each work, or use the buttons along the top to walk straight to it. “Walk” switches to a free camera that circles the work you are looking at.

Image and model credits

Architecture is modelled approximately to true scale (nave about 186 m long, dome 41.5 m across). Photographs are openly licensed from Wikimedia Commons; see credits.
